TummyBook — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

The short version: your gut diary — meals, bowel movements, symptoms, meds, notes, diet phases and challenges — lives on your phone. There is no TummyBook account or cloud, no ads and no usage analytics, and we never see your health information. Your health data never leaves the phone unless you export it (on iPhone, your phone’s own backup may also include it — see below). Each time it opens, TummyBook checks your unlock status with RevenueCat — the store receipt and an anonymous ID, never your health data.

What we collect

No health information, ever. TummyBook has no accounts, no usage analytics, no ads, no crash-reporting service and no tracking of any kind. Everything you enter — meals and foods, bowel movements, symptom scores, meds and supplements, notes, diet phases and reintroduction challenges, and the optional name and details you add to your report — is stored only on your device.

The app does connect to the internet for the optional one-time unlock: each time it opens or comes back to the screen it checks your unlock status with RevenueCat, it loads the unlock’s price from the App Store or Google Play, and buying or restoring the unlock goes through the store and RevenueCat (store receipt and an anonymous app user ID). We never receive, store or sell any health information.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter is stored locally on your device, not on any server of ours. It leaves the app only when YOU export it — the PDF report, a CSV file or a backup file — and send or save it yourself, for example to your doctor or dietitian.

What your phone’s own backup does depends on the phone:

  • iPhone: Your iPhone’s own backup (iCloud or your computer) may include it. That backup belongs to your Apple account, not to TummyBook. The app itself stores nothing in iCloud.
  • Android: Google’s phone backup does not include it — use “Save a backup file” before you change phones.

Third parties

Your entries are never sent to anyone. TummyBook includes one third-party SDK, RevenueCat, used only for the optional one-time unlock after the 7-day free trial: to check your unlock status each time the app opens, to show its price and to validate a purchase or a restore. The purchase itself is processed by Apple or Google Play; RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt and an anonymous app user ID, plus the technical details its software sends to talk to the store (such as the app and system version, and possibly a device identifier). RevenueCat also uses the purchase records to show us sales totals. It never receives your meals, stool entries, symptoms, meds, notes, name or any health information, and none of this is used for tracking or advertising.

Permissions

TummyBook asks for no permissions: no camera, photos, location, contacts, notifications or health data access. Files are only read when you choose a backup file to restore.

Store privacy labels

Apple App Store: "Data Not Linked to You" — Purchases (purchase history), used for app functionality (the unlock) and analytics (sales totals in RevenueCat), and Identifiers (anonymous user ID, device ID), used for app functionality. None of it is used for tracking. Google Play Data safety: purchase history and device or other IDs, collected for app functionality and analytics, not shared and never used for tracking. TummyBook never sends health information anywhere.

Your rights

Your data is yours. “Delete all my data” in Settings erases every entry, your meds and supplements, phases, challenges, symptoms list, tags, report details and settings, and the export and backup files the app kept on the phone. It keeps only your purchase status and the dates of your free trial, so the unlock is not lost and the trial is not restarted — neither contains health information. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the phone. Copies outside the app — files you exported and shared, or an iPhone backup — are not reached by either; delete them where they are. You can export your diary at any time from the Report tab or Settings.

Not medical advice

TummyBook records what you enter. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical or diet advice. Talk to your doctor or dietitian before changing your diet or medicines.

Children

TummyBook is not directed to children and collects no personal information from anyone.
