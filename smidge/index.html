Smidge

Clean a smidge at a time.

Can't start? Tap what you see. Smidge turns the mess into tiny 5-minute steps, one at a time. It's a cleaning helper made with ADHD brains in mind: no account, no ads, no AI, and no streaks to lose.

Quick guide

  • Tap I can't start → pick a space → tap what you see.
  • Do one step. Tap Done. Stop anytime; it still counts.
  • Too big? Tap Make it smaller.
  • Guests coming? Pick 15, 30 or 60 minutes.
  • Decluttering? Decide one thing at a time: keep, donate, toss.

Questions

Does it sync?

No, on purpose. Your data stays on your phone. Use Settings → Save a backup to move it to a new phone, then Restore a backup there.

Is Smidge a medical app?

No. It's a cleaning helper made with ADHD brains in mind.

Why no AI?

Steps come from simple built-in lists. Smidge never uploads your spaces, steps or photos: they only leave your phone when you share them or save a backup.

Can someone else at home use it?

Yes, on the same phone. There are no accounts, so there's nothing to add. Smidge doesn't sync between phones; use Share as text to send someone a list.

Why no streaks?

Missing a day shouldn't feel like failing. Every smidge counts.

Why didn't the timer make a sound?

The sound comes from a notification. Turn on Settings → Sound when time's up, and allow notifications.

The sound or the daily nudge came late.

Android sometimes delays notifications to save battery. The timer on the screen is always right.

How do I restore my purchase?

If you bought Smidge Pro, open Settings → Restore purchase while signed in to the same Google Play account you used to buy it.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete all my data. Details are in the privacy policy.
