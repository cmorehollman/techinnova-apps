Smidge — Privacy Policy

Smidge: ADHD Cleaning App · Last updated: September 29, 2026

The short version

Your spaces, steps, photos and history stay on your phone. Smidge has no account, no ads, no analytics and no AI. We never see what you clean, what you keep or what you photograph.

What we collect

Your cleaning data: nothing. Smidge has no accounts, no login, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. There is no Smidge server that receives your data.

Smidge may offer one optional one-time upgrade ("Smidge Pro"). In versions where that purchase is not turned on, no purchase data exists. When it is turned on and you buy it, the payment is processed by Google Play and the purchase is checked by RevenueCat (see Third parties). Smidge never sees your card or payment details.

Where your data lives

Everything you create in Smidge is stored in a database inside the app's private storage on your phone: your spaces, the steps and sessions you do, your routines and their checkmarks, your declutter decisions (and any item names you type), your Maybe box and Take-out list, the thoughts you park for later, and your settings. Before & after photos are saved in the app's private folder on your phone.

Your data only leaves your phone when YOU send it:

  • Share as text: a list you send through your phone's share menu (a message, an email, a note…).
  • Print: a checklist you send to your phone's print screen, where you pick a printer or save a PDF.
  • Export CSV: your Done list as a file you share or save where you choose.
  • Share a photo: a before or after photo you send through the share menu.
  • Save a backup: a backup file (it includes your photos) that you save wherever you choose. If you save it to a cloud storage app, that app's own privacy terms apply; Smidge never uploads anything itself.

Third parties

Your spaces, steps, photos and history are never sent to anyone. Smidge includes one third-party service, RevenueCat, used only for the optional one-time purchase. When the purchase is turned on, RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt, an anonymous app user ID and a device identifier, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. This is not linked to your name or email, is never used for tracking or advertising, and is never sold. In versions where the purchase is off, Smidge does not start RevenueCat at all.

After you have finished sessions on a few different days (never on your first day), Smidge may ask Google Play once to show its standard rating window. Any rating or review you leave goes to Google Play, not to us.

Smidge does not use your location, contacts, microphone, calendar or files. To restore a backup, you pick the file yourself on your phone's own screen; Smidge can only read the file you pick. The app package also lists network state and wake lock: they come with the standard libraries Smidge uses for purchases and notifications. Data safety summary

Data collected: purchase history and a device or other identifier, only for the optional purchase, used only for app functionality, not shared, not used to track you, and encrypted in transit. Your spaces, steps, routines, photos, notes and history: not collected, not shared.

Not a medical app

Smidge is a cleaning helper made with ADHD brains in mind. It does not ask for, store or share any health information.

How to delete your data

Your data is yours, and you can remove it at any time:

  1. On your phone: open Smidge → Settings → Delete all my data. After two confirmations it permanently erases every space, routine, session, declutter item, parked note, photo and setting on the phone, and cancels any scheduled notifications. The only thing it keeps is whether you bought Pro, so you don't lose your purchase. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from the phone.
  2. Files you shared or saved: backups, CSV files, PDFs, photos and lists you sent outside the app are yours; delete them where you keep them.
  3. The purchase record: if you bought Pro, email us at cmorehollman@gmail.com and we will request deletion of the anonymous purchase receipt held by RevenueCat. No name or email is attached to it. Your Google Play order history is managed in your Google account.

Children

Smidge is made for adults. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. If a future version ever sends your spaces, steps, photos or any other data off your phone, we will say so here before that version is published.

Resumen en español

Tus espacios, pasos, fotos e historial se quedan en tu teléfono. Smidge no tiene cuentas, anuncios, analítica, IA ni rastreo, y nosotros nunca vemos lo que limpias, lo que guardas ni lo que fotografías. Todo se guarda en el almacenamiento privado de la app, y solo sale de tu teléfono cuando tú lo compartes como texto, lo imprimes, exportas el CSV, compartes una foto o guardas un respaldo (que incluye tus fotos) donde tú elijas. La única pieza de terceros es RevenueCat, para la compra opcional única de Pro: cuando está activa, recibe el recibo de compra, un identificador anónimo y un identificador del dispositivo, por HTTPS y sin rastreo. Si la compra no está activa, Smidge ni siquiera inicia RevenueCat.

La cámara se pide solo al tocar "Tomar una foto" para las fotos de antes y después; las notificaciones, solo si activas el sonido del temporizador o el aviso diario. Smidge es una ayuda para limpiar, no una app médica: no pide ni guarda datos de salud.

Para borrar todo: Smidge → Ajustes → Borrar todos mis datos (borra espacios, rutinas, sesiones, cosas por ordenar, notas, fotos y ajustes, y cancela los avisos programados; solo conserva si compraste Pro). Desinstalar la app también borra sus datos del teléfono. Si compraste Pro, escríbenos a cmorehollman@gmail.com para pedir que se borre el recibo anónimo.
