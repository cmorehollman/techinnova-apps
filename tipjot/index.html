How Tipjot is organized

  • Log: enter a shift (cash, card, tip-out, hours, sales and a note). Every save is kept on the phone.
  • Ledger: your shifts by week, month and year. Tap a shift to edit or delete it.
  • Hand-off: the monthly tip report for each employer, and the PDF and CSV for your accountant.
  • More: jobs, Safekeeping (backup, check, restore, erase), Check a file, How it works and About.

Common questions

How do I move my tips to a new phone? In Safekeeping, tap Back up to a file and save it where you can reach it, for example in Files. On the new phone, install Tipjot and use Restore with that file. Restore replaces everything, all or nothing.

How do I know a backup is good? Use Check a backup. It compares the file with the phone and changes nothing.

What is the fingerprint? A code (SHA-256) made from the data in a file. Check a file shows whether a CSV that Tipjot made still matches its code, and lets you compare the code printed on a PDF with that CSV. A match says the data equals the code; it says nothing about who made the file.

I bought Tipjot on another phone. On the purchase screen, tap Restore my purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID.

What happens after the 7-day trial? Without the purchase you cannot log new shifts, but your history, reports, CSV and PDF, file checks, backup, restore and erase keep working.

Does Tipjot work out my taxes? No. Tipjot shows your totals. It never works out tax, a deduction or savings. Not tax advice: ask a tax professional about your situation.
