Tipjot

A simple tip log for servers and bartenders

No accountWorks offlineNo ads

Tipjot keeps your cash tips, card tips, tip-out and hours for every shift, shows what you make per hour, and prints a clean tip record. Everything is stored on your phone. This page explains how it works and how to reach us.

Log a shift

  1. The first time you open Tipjot, add your first job (for example "Server") with its hourly wage (it can be 0), your pay period and the day your week starts.
  2. On Home, tap Log shift. Pick the job, the date (before 5 AM a new shift is dated yesterday, and the form says so), and the hours: tap 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 or 10 h, type them (6.5 or 6:30), or use clock in and clock out. Overnight shifts stay on the day they started.
  3. Type your cash tips, card tips and tip-out, then tap Save shift. Net tips = cash + card − tip-out.
  4. Also on the form, optional: the amount you reported to your employer, and — once you type a tip-out — who you tipped out. Under "More details": sales (for your tip % of sales), the hourly wage for that shift, and a note.
  5. Tap any shift in Home or History to edit it. Deleted one by mistake? Tap Undo right away.

Pay periods, jobs and reports

  • Pay period: weekly, every 2 weeks (pick the day your current pay period started), twice a month or monthly — in Settings › Pay period. History groups your shifts by pay period.
  • Several jobs: Settings › Jobs › Add job. Each job has its own default wage and color. A job with shifts can be archived (it stays in your history and reports); a job with no shifts can be deleted.
  • Reports: this week, this pay period, this month, last month, this year, last year or any dates you choose. With two or more jobs you can look at one job at a time.

Tip record (PDF), CSV and backups

  • Tip record (PDF): Reports › pick a range (and a job, if you like) › Tip record (PDF). Your phone's share sheet opens: print it, save it to Files or send it.
  • CSV: Reports › Export CSV. It opens in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets.
  • Backup file: Settings › Your data › Save a backup file. Keep the file somewhere safe, like Files or your email, then tap Yes, saved. Each file has the day it was made in its name (for example Tipjot-backup-2026-09-28.json). Tipjot reminds you after 30 new shifts without a saved backup.

Questions

How do I move my tips to a new phone?

On the old phone: Settings › Save a backup file, and keep the file. On the new phone: install Tipjot and tap Moving from another phone? Restore a backup file on the first screen (or Settings › Restore from a backup file later) and pick the file — the newest one, by the date in its name. Before anything is replaced, Tipjot shows how many shifts are on the phone and warns you if the file is older; if the phone had shifts, you can tap Undo restore right after. If you bought the one-time unlock, tap Restore purchase in Settings too.

On iPhone, your phone's own backup (iCloud or a computer) may also include Tipjot's data. On Android, Tipjot is not included in the device backup, so the backup file is the way to move.

Does Tipjot cost anything?

The download is free, with no ads and no account. If your version of Tipjot shows a free trial, the trial lasts 7 days; after it, logging new shifts takes a one-time purchase — no subscription. Your history, editing, reports, PDF and CSV exports and backups stay free whether you buy or not. If your version shows no trial, every feature is included.

I bought the unlock and reinstalled the app. How do I get it back?

Settings › Restore purchase. The purchase belongs to your App Store or Google Play account, so you are not charged again.

What does "Reported" mean in my totals?

It is the amount you entered as reported to your employer for a shift. Totals add it up only for the shifts where you entered one, and say how many shifts that is (for example "22 of 43 shifts").

Does Tipjot tell me how much to report or pay in taxes?

No. Tipjot is a personal record-keeping app. All amounts are entered by you. It does not give tax, legal or financial advice. Tipjot is not affiliated with the IRS or any employer.

How do I delete everything?

Settings › Your data › Delete all my data (it asks twice), or uninstall the app. Save a backup file first if you want a copy.
