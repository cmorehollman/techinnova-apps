Tipjot privacy policy

Effective November 4, 2026

The short version: Tipjot has no sign-up, no server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The jobs, shifts and tips you enter stay on your phone unless you share them, as a report, a CSV or a backup file you choose to send or save. Some phones can also copy app data on their own, as explained below. The app sends us no data about you. If you buy Tipjot, the app store gives us a sales report, explained below.

What Tipjot keeps, and where

Everything you enter is stored in Tipjot's database on your phone:

  • the jobs you name;
  • each shift: its work day, hours, cash tips, card tips, tip-out, optional sales and a note;
  • which monthly reports you marked as given to your employer;
  • your settings, and small app notes such as the day you first opened Tipjot (for the 7-day trial) and when you last made a backup file.

Tipjot has no server, and the app does not send what you enter to us or to anyone else. It leaves the phone if you share it. Separately, some phones can copy app data on their own, in a phone backup or when you move to a new phone. That depends on the phone and its settings, and any such copy is kept under the rules of the service you use for it, not by us. To move your tips, use a backup file.

Files you choose to share

Tipjot can make a monthly tip report (PDF), a tip log (PDF and CSV) and a backup file. They are made on your phone and go only where you send or save them through your phone's share menu. Once a file leaves Tipjot, its copy is yours to keep or delete. These files are not encrypted: anyone who gets a file can read it.

The fingerprint

Every CSV, PDF and backup file carries a fingerprint (SHA-256): a code made from the data in the file. If one number or letter changes, the code changes. Check a file and Check a backup show whether a CSV or backup file Tipjot made still matches its code. Check a file also lets you compare the code printed on a PDF with its CSV. A match means the data equals the code. It says nothing about who made the file.

Backup and restore

A backup file holds your jobs, shifts, report marks and settings, plus its fingerprint. Restore replaces everything on the phone with the file, all or nothing. If the file is damaged or from a newer version of Tipjot, nothing changes.

Purchases

After the 7-day trial, one purchase through your phone's app store lets you keep logging new shifts. The store handles the payment. Tipjot uses the store's own purchase system on the phone and no purchase service of its own, so we never receive your card or payment details. The app talks to the store only to show the price, make or check your purchase and, once after your first file, ask the store to show its rating window. It never gives the store what you enter.

Like any developer, we get the store's sales reports, install counts, usage and crash statistics, and any review you choose to post. A sales report can include an order number, the date, the price, the device model and the buyer's city, state, postal code and country, depending on the store. It does not include anything you enter in Tipjot. We use these to count sales and see how the app is doing. The store's own privacy policy covers the purchase.

Erase everything

Use Erase everything in Safekeeping (in More) to remove every job, shift and mark from this phone. Some small app notes, such as the day you first opened Tipjot (for the 7-day trial), stay until you delete the app. Deleting the app also deletes its data from the phone. If you saved backup files or shared reports, delete those copies yourself; we have no copy. If your phone made its own copy, that copy stays under the rules of the service that keeps it, and restoring it can bring the data back. We hold nothing you enter in Tipjot, so there is nothing of that for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question about a store's sales report, write to us.

Not tax advice

Figures are entered by you. Tipjot keeps a record. It is not tax advice and is not affiliated with the IRS or any employer.

Children

Tipjot is not directed at children under 13, and the app sends us no data from anyone.
