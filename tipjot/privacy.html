Tipjot — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

The short version: the shifts, tips, jobs and notes you enter stay on your phone (and in your phone's own iCloud or computer backup, if you use one). Tipjot has no account, no server or cloud of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Tipjot sends your data nowhere except in files you choose to share (a tip record PDF, a CSV or a backup file). If the app offers its one-time unlock, the purchase is checked with RevenueCat using an anonymous ID — never your tips.

Tipjot is made by TechInnova Solutions LLC. This page explains what the app does with your data. It applies to Tipjot for iPhone (bundle ID com.techinnova.tipjot) and for Android (package com.techinnova.tipjot).

What the app does

Tipjot is a personal tip log. You enter your jobs (for example "Server"), and for each shift the date, the hours worked, cash tips, card tips, tips you paid out to others, and optional details: sales, the hourly wage for that shift, the amount you reported to your employer, who you tipped out, and a note. The app adds these up into totals and reports and can build a tip record PDF or a CSV file for a date range you choose.

What we collect

Your tips, shifts, jobs, notes and settings: nothing. They are stored only in a database inside the app on your phone. We have no account system and no server that receives them. The app contains no analytics, no advertising SDK and no tracking of any kind.

Where your data lives

DataWhere it isHow long
Jobs, shifts, amounts, hours, notes, pay-period settings and the optional name printed on the tip record A local database in the app's private storage on your phone. Until you edit or delete it, tap Settings › "Delete all my data", or uninstall the app.
Tip record PDFs, CSV files and backup files you create A temporary copy in the app's private storage, handed to your phone's share sheet. Where you send it (Files, email, a message) is your choice, and that service's own privacy policy then applies. The app's copies are deleted by "Delete all my data". Copies you already shared are not affected.

Phone backups. On iPhone, your phone's own backup (iCloud or a computer) may include Tipjot's data, as it does for most apps; Tipjot does not add any cloud of its own. On Android, the app is excluded from the device backup, so use Settings › "Save a backup file" before changing phones.

Purchases

Tipjot may offer a one-time unlock ("Tipjot Unlocked") after a 7-day free trial. When it does, the purchase is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) — Tipjot never sees your card or bank details — and it is validated by RevenueCat, the only third-party service in the app. When the app opens, it checks your unlock status with RevenueCat and asks the store for the price. RevenueCat receives the store receipt, an anonymous app user ID generated on your device, and the technical details its software sends with each check: the app and system versions, the device model, your phone's language and store country, and, on iPhone, the identifier Apple gives each app maker for your device (not the advertising identifier). This is not linked to your name or email, is not used for advertising and is not sold. We see purchases in RevenueCat's sales reports, without your name. None of your tips, shifts or notes is ever sent. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

In a version of Tipjot that does not offer the unlock, the app makes no connection to RevenueCat or any other service.

Permissions

  • No camera, microphone, contacts, location or photo library access.
  • Restoring a backup opens your phone's own file picker; the app only reads the file you choose.
  • The app uses the internet only for the purchase checks described above. The "Support" and "Privacy policy" links in Settings open these web pages in your browser.

App Store privacy label

  • Version without the in-app purchase: Data Not Collected.
  • Version with the in-app purchase: Data Not Linked to You — Purchases (your purchase history, used for app functionality and for our sales reports) and Identifiers (the anonymous user ID and the device identifier described above, used for app functionality). Not used for tracking.

Children

Tipjot is meant for working adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your choices

  • Delete everything on the phone: Settings › "Delete all my data" (it asks twice), or uninstall the app. Your purchase is not deleted and can be restored.
  • Export: Reports › "Tip record (PDF)" or "Export CSV", and Settings › "Save a backup file".
  • The anonymous purchase record: in the version with the in-app purchase, Settings › About shows its anonymous purchase ID. Email us that ID and we will ask RevenueCat to delete the matching record. This also removes the ability to restore the purchase on another phone.

Because we hold none of your tip data, requests to access, correct or delete personal data (for example under California, EU/EEA or UK law) are fulfilled by the steps above.

Not advice

Tipjot is a personal record-keeping app. All amounts are entered by you. It does not give tax, legal or financial advice and is not affiliated with the IRS or any employer.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting something new, this page will change first, the effective date above will move, and the update notes will say so.
