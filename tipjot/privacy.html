Tipjot privacy policy

Effective October 25, 2026

The short version: Tipjot has no account, no cloud of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The jobs, shifts and tips you enter stay on your phone unless you share them, as a report, a CSV or a backup file you choose to send or save. We collect no data about you.

What Tipjot keeps, and where

Everything you enter is stored only in Tipjot's database on your iPhone:

  • the jobs you name;
  • each shift: its work day, hours, cash tips, card tips, tip-out, optional sales and a note;
  • which monthly reports you marked as given to your employer;
  • your settings, and small app notes such as the day you first opened Tipjot (for the 7-day trial) and when you last made a backup file.

Tipjot has no server. Nothing you enter is sent to us or to anyone else unless you share it. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, that backup may include Tipjot's data, as it does for other apps.

Files you choose to share

Tipjot can make a monthly tip report (PDF), a tip log (PDF and CSV) and a backup file. They are made on your phone and go only where you send or save them through the iPhone share sheet. Once a file leaves Tipjot, its copy is yours to keep or delete. These files are not encrypted: anyone who gets a file can read it.

The fingerprint

Every CSV, PDF and backup file carries a fingerprint (SHA-256): a code made from the data in the file. With Check a file and Check a backup, Tipjot can show whether a file it made still matches that code. A match means the data equals the code. It says nothing about who made the file.

Backup and restore

A backup file holds everything on the phone, plus its fingerprint. Restore replaces everything on the phone with the file, all or nothing. If the file is damaged or from a newer version of Tipjot, nothing changes.

Purchases

After the 7-day trial, one purchase through the App Store lets you keep logging new shifts. Apple handles the payment. Tipjot uses Apple's StoreKit on the phone and no purchase service of its own, so we receive no payment details and no information about you.

Erase everything

Use Erase everything in Safekeeping (in More) to remove every job, shift and mark from this phone. Deleting the app also deletes its data. If you saved backup files or shared reports, delete those copies yourself; we have no copy. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Not tax advice

Figures are entered by you. Tipjot keeps a record. It is not tax advice and is not affiliated with the IRS or any employer.

Children

Tipjot is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
