Keelcard support

A weekly DBT diary card for iPhone, laid out like the paper one.

Not a substitute for therapy. Keelcard is a place to keep your diary card. It is not therapy or crisis care. If you are in crisis in the US or Canada, call or text 988. If you are in danger right now, call 911. Elsewhere, call your local emergency number.

Common questions

How do I share my card with my therapist?

Open the session view and tap Share PDF. The PDF uses the paper card's layout (Letter, landscape). You can also print it or show the card on screen during your session.

Who can see my card?

No account. Stays on your phone unless you share it. Keelcard has no ads and no analytics, and the app doesn't send your entries anywhere. Keelcard has no passcode of its own, so anyone who can unlock your phone can open it.

How do I move my card to a new phone?

From the menu, save a backup file and keep it somewhere you choose (for example, in Files). On the new phone, install Keelcard and use Restore with that file. Restore brings back everything in the file. If the file is damaged, nothing on your phone changes. The backup file isn't encrypted, so keep it somewhere private.

Can I change the rows on my card?

Yes. In Build my card you can rename, add, reorder or hide rows and skills. Hidden rows keep their past numbers.

Can I get a reminder?

Yes, an optional reminder the evening before your session day. iOS asks your permission first. It is a notification set on your phone, it can show on your lock screen, and you can turn it off at any time. Nothing is sent to us.

What does the Support button do?

It shows 988 (call or text) and 911. Calls and texts use your phone, and the 988 chat opens in your browser. Keelcard never calls anyone for you.

Does it work on iPad?

Keelcard is made for iPhone. On an iPad it runs as an iPhone app.

How do I erase everything?

Use Erase in the menu. It deletes every card and setting on this phone. Deleting the app also removes its data. If you saved a backup file or shared a PDF, delete those copies too.

Is there a subscription?

No. Keelcard is paid once, when you download it. There is nothing to unlock inside the app.
