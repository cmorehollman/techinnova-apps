Keelcard

A DBT diary card on your phone

If you are not safe right now

Call or text 988 (US and Canada), call 911, or call your local emergency number. Keelcard is a personal diary card. It is not therapy, medical advice or a crisis service, and nobody at Keelcard reads what you write.

Keelcard lets you log urges, actions, emotions and the skills you used — as often as you need, on any day — and share a weekly PDF laid out like the paper card with your therapist. There is no account to sign in to and no ads. This page explains how it works and how to reach us.

Contact

Email cmorehollman@gmail.com. Please don’t send your entries or anything private — we don’t need them to help, and we never see your card.

Questions

How do I share my card with my therapist?

Open the Week tab and tap Share this week (PDF). For last week or several weeks at once (up to 12), tap More weeks or CSV…. The front of each card has your rows × 7 days; the back has your skills × 7 days, then the week’s notes. Send it with Mail, Messages, AirDrop, Print or Files.

My therapist uses a different card. Can I change the rows?

Yes: Settings › Edit rows. Tap a name to rename it, move rows up or down, remove them, add your own (0–5, 0–7, Yes/No or a count) and choose which ones print on the PDF. Skills are edited the same way in Settings › Edit skills.

How do I move my card to a new phone?

On the old phone: Settings › Your data › Save a backup file, and keep that file somewhere you can reach from the new phone (email it to yourself or save it to Files). On the new phone: Restore from a backup file on the first screen, or later in Settings › Your data. Restoring replaces the card on that phone with the one in the file.

Where is my data?

In Keelcard’s own storage on your phone. There is no Keelcard account or server. Your entries leave the app only when you export them (PDF, CSV or a backup file). On iPhone, your phone’s own backup (iCloud or your computer) may include your card. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete everything?

Settings › Your data › Delete all my data erases every check-in, your rows, skills and contacts on the phone. Files you already exported or shared are not reached — delete them where they are.

I turned on the app lock and Face ID doesn’t work

Your phone’s passcode always works as well. If the phone no longer has a passcode or biometrics set up, the lock steps aside so you are never locked out of your own card. The 988 buttons work on the lock screen without unlocking.

Restore purchase

If your version of Keelcard has the one-time unlock and you already bought it (on this phone or another one with the same App Store or Google Play account), tap Restore purchase in Settings › Your plan or on the unlock screen. You are not charged again. Viewing and editing your card, the PDF, the CSV and backup files never need the unlock.

Does Keelcard teach DBT skills?

No. Keelcard is where you record them; your program teaches them. It lists skill names only, and it never interprets your numbers.
