Keelcard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This policy is for versions of Keelcard that offer the one-time unlock (Settings › Your plan shows an Unlock or Restore purchase button). Versions with nothing to buy have their own privacy policy. Each version’s Privacy policy link in Settings opens the one that matches it.

The short version: your diary card — urges, actions, emotions, skills, notes and support contacts — lives on your phone. There is no Keelcard account or cloud, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never see what you write. Your entries never leave the phone unless you export them (on iPhone, your phone’s own backup may also include them — see below). Each time it opens, Keelcard checks your unlock status with RevenueCat — the store receipt and an anonymous ID, never your entries.

What we collect

Nothing you write. Keelcard has no accounts, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no ads and no tracking. Your check-ins, card rows, skills, notes, the optional name on your PDF and the support contacts you add are stored on your device, in the app’s own storage (and in the backup files you choose to save).

The app does connect to the internet for one thing: the optional one-time unlock. Each time it opens or comes back to the screen it checks your unlock status with RevenueCat and loads the unlock’s price from the App Store or Google Play; buying or restoring the unlock goes through the store and RevenueCat (see Third parties). While the store is not set up or can’t be reached, the app keeps working and nothing is locked.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter is stored locally on your device, not on any server of ours. Keelcard does not use iCloud storage, CloudKit or any cloud of its own. Your entries leave the app only when YOU export them — the weekly PDF, a CSV or a backup file — and share them yourself, for example with your therapist.

What your phone’s own backup does depends on the phone:

  • iPhone: Your iPhone’s own backup (iCloud or your computer) may include your card. That backup belongs to your Apple account, not to Keelcard.
  • Android: Android’s own backup and its phone-to-phone transfer do not include your card — use “Save a backup file” before you change phones.

Optional features on your phone

  • App lock: if you turn it on, Keelcard asks your phone to confirm it’s you with Face ID, Touch ID, a fingerprint or your device passcode. The check happens entirely on your device; Keelcard never receives your face, fingerprint or passcode.
  • Daily reminder: if you turn it on, your phone shows one local notification a day (“A moment for today’s card?”). It never includes anything you wrote. There are no push notifications.
  • Support contacts and 988: the Call and Text buttons open your phone’s dialer or messages app with the number filled in. Keelcard never calls or texts anyone by itself and never contacts anyone for you.

Third parties

Your entries are never sent to anyone. Keelcard includes one third-party SDK, RevenueCat, used only for the optional one-time unlock after the 7-day free trial: to check your unlock status each time the app opens, to show its price and to validate a purchase or a restore. The purchase itself is processed by Apple or Google Play; RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt, an anonymous app user ID and a device identifier. It never receives your check-ins, notes, contacts or name, and none of this is used for tracking or advertising.

Store privacy labels

Apple App Store: “Data Not Linked to You” — Purchases (purchase history) and Identifiers (user ID, device ID), used only for app functionality and never for tracking. Google Play Data safety: purchase history and device or other IDs, collected only for app functionality (the unlock), not shared and never used for tracking.

Your rights and deleting your data

Your data is yours. Export it any time from the Week tab or Settings. “Delete all my data” in Settings erases every check-in, your card rows, skills, support contacts and settings, the export and backup files the app kept on the phone, and the daily reminder. It keeps only your purchase status, the dates of your free trial, how many cards you have shared and whether the app already asked you for a store review, so the unlock is not lost, the trial is not restarted and the review request is never repeated — none of them contains anything you wrote. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the phone. Copies outside the app — files you exported and shared, or an iPhone backup — are not reached by either; delete them where they are.

Not medical advice

Keelcard is a personal diary card. It is not therapy, medical advice or a crisis service, and it does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you are in danger, call or text 988 (US and Canada), call 911, or call your local emergency number. Talk with your therapist or doctor about what you log, and before making any decision about your care.

Children

Keelcard never collects anything you write, from anyone, including children. The only data it handles is what the one-time unlock needs: the store receipt and the anonymous IDs that RevenueCat uses to check the unlock (see Third parties). Purchases on a child’s device follow the family settings of the App Store or Google Play (for example Ask to Buy).
