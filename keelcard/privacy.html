Keelcard privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026 · Keelcard by TechInnova Solutions LLC

The short version: No account. Stays on your phone unless you share it. We don't collect, see or store anything you enter.

What we collect

Nothing. Keelcard has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data. The app itself sends nothing to our servers or anyone else's.

You buy Keelcard once, at download, through the App Store. Apple handles that purchase under Apple's privacy policy; we don't receive your payment details.

Where your card lives

Everything you enter (your rows, the numbers you pick, skills, notes and settings) is stored on your iPhone, in the app's own storage.

  • Your iPhone's own backup: if you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, that backup may include Keelcard's data. Your iPhone settings control this, not Keelcard.
  • PDF: when you tap Share PDF or Print, you choose where it goes (for example, an email to your therapist or a printer).
  • Backup file: when you save a Keelcard backup, you choose where to keep it. The backup file and the PDF are not encrypted: anyone who gets the file can read it.
  • No passcode of its own: anyone who can unlock your phone can open Keelcard.

Once you send a PDF by email or message, that copy is covered by the email or messaging service you used. Keelcard is not a medical record service.

Reminders and links

The optional reminder is a notification scheduled on your phone. iOS asks your permission first; it can appear on your lock screen, and you can turn it off at any time in Keelcard or in iOS Settings. Nothing about it is sent to us.

Calls and texts to 988 or 911 use your phone. The 988 chat link and links to this page open in your browser; that connection is yours, not Keelcard's.

Deleting your data

Use Erase everything in the app's menu to delete every card and setting on this phone. Deleting the app also deletes its data. If you saved backup files or shared PDFs, delete those copies yourself; we have no copy. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side.

Children

Keelcard is not directed to children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

App Store privacy label

Data Not Collected. No tracking.

Not a substitute for therapy

Keelcard is a place to keep your diary card. It is not therapy or crisis care. If you are in crisis in the US or Canada, call or text 988. If you are in danger right now, call 911. Elsewhere, call your local emergency number.

This website

These support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep basic visit logs under GitHub's own privacy statement. The pages have no ads, analytics or tracking code.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If the app ever starts collecting data, the App Store privacy label and this page will say so first.
