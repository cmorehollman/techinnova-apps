Inspector Owl — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

The short version: everything you enter — jobs, findings, photos, templates and reports — stays on your phone. We have no account system and no server of our own, so we never see or receive it.

What we collect

Your inspection data: nothing. Inspector Owl has no accounts, no login, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no tracking of any kind. There is currently no purchase to make in the app — it is free while in early access — so no purchase or payment data is collected either.

Where your data lives

Every job, section, finding, photo, custom template and defect you add is stored locally on your device, in the app's own on-device database. It never leaves your phone unless YOU choose to export a JSON backup or share a generated PDF report yourself — the same as sharing any other file from your phone.

Camera and photos

If you attach a photo to a finding, Inspector Owl asks for camera or photo library permission to do that one thing. The photo is stored locally with your inspection record; it is never uploaded to us and never seen by anyone unless you include it when you share your own report.

Voice dictation

You can dictate a finding using the microphone button on your own keyboard (for example Gboard). The speech-to-text is performed by your keyboard app under its own permissions and policy — Inspector Owl never records audio, never receives audio, and matches the resulting text against its built-in defect library entirely on your device.

"Coming soon" features

The app shows a disabled preview chip — "AI polish" — for a feature we are building next. It is not active: tapping it does not send any text anywhere or collect any data today.

Third parties

Your entries are never sent to anyone. The app bundles the RevenueCat purchase SDK for a future paid plan, but no RevenueCat project is connected yet (billing is fully off) — no purchase or device data is sent to RevenueCat while that remains the case. This page will be updated, and the in-app and app-store data disclosures corrected, before any purchase option is turned on.

Data safety summary

Data collected: none. Data shared with third parties: none. Everything you enter is stored only on your device.

Your control

Because your content lives only on your device, you are always in control de it. Export a full copy any time from Settings → "Export everything (JSON)", or erase everything at once with "Delete ALL my data" — or simply uninstall the app.

Children

Inspector Owl is a professional tool for home inspectors and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a future update adds a purchase option, optional cloud backup, or any new data flow, we will update this page and the app store data disclosures before that feature ships.
