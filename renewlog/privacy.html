RenewLog — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

The short version: your GLP-1 record — shots, weight, side effects, lifestyle — never leaves your phone. RenewLog has no account, no cloud and no ads, and we never see your health information.

What we collect

Nothing. RenewLog has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking of any kind. The current version makes no network requests at all: your doses, weight entries, side effects and lifestyle log exist only on your device. We never receive, store or sell any health information.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter is stored locally on your device. It never leaves your phone unless YOU export it (the Renewal Packet PDF or a CSV) and share it yourself — with your doctor, your insurer or anyone you choose.

Third parties

Your entries are never sent to anyone. The current version is free and talks to no servers. If a future version adds an optional one-time unlock, the purchase would be processed by Google Play or Apple and validated by RevenueCat (receipt + anonymous ID only, never your health data) — and this policy will be updated first.

Store privacy labels

Apple App Store: "Data Not Collected". Google Play Data safety: no data collected, no data shared. Health information never leaves the device.

Your rights

Your data is yours. Use "Delete all my data" in Settings (or simply delete the app) and everything is gone permanently. Export it any time from Settings or the Packet tab.

Not medical advice

RenewLog is a personal logbook. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it cannot promise any insurance outcome. Always talk to your healthcare provider.
