Tillglow

Count down to the days that matter, right on your home screen. No ads. No account. Your dates stay on your phone.

What Tillglow does

  • Home-screen widgets (small, square or wide) that are built to update themselves at midnight, with a live timer in the last 48 hours.
  • Countdowns to any date and count-ups from any date since 1900: Christmas, a wedding, retirement, a trip, a birthday.
  • Show the wait in days, weeks & days, months & days, or weekdays only (Mon–Fri).
  • 16 templates, every-year repeats, reminders, notes, emoji, color styles and your own photo.
  • Backup file, CSV export and "Delete all my data" in Settings. English and Spanish, light and dark mode.

FAQ

How do I add a widget?

Open the Widgets tab and tap Add widget to home screen, then confirm on your phone's screen. You can also long-press an empty spot on your home screen → Widgets → Tillglow. Tap a widget to open its countdown. To change what a widget shows, tap Change next to it in the Widgets tab.

Does it sync between phones?

No — on purpose. Your dates never leave your phone.

Got a new phone?

In Settings, tap Save a backup copy and keep the file in your files or cloud storage. On the new phone, open Tillglow and tap Restore from a backup. If you bought Tillglow Pro, then tap Restore purchase. Widgets are put back on the new home screen by hand.

Why is my widget a day behind?

Android can hold the midnight update while your phone is asleep; it catches up as soon as you wake the screen. If it stays behind, open Tillglow once.

My widget isn't updating

Open Tillglow once after installing or updating it. On Samsung phones: Settings › Battery (on some models Device care › Battery) › Background usage limits — make sure Tillglow isn't in "Deep sleeping apps". Some phones delay the midnight update by a few minutes to save battery. In the Widgets tab, Refresh widgets now redraws them.

Why did a reminder arrive late?

To save battery, Android can deliver a reminder up to an hour after the time you set, and sometimes later while Battery Saver is on or the phone is asleep. Tillglow uses the normal, battery-friendly kind of reminder and never asks for special alarm permissions. If the hour matters, set the reminder a little earlier.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete all my data. It erases your countdowns, reminders, photos and settings from the phone and resets your widgets. See the privacy policy for details.
