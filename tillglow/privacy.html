Tillglow — Privacy Policy

Tillglow: Countdown Widget · Last updated: September 29, 2026

The short version

Your countdowns, notes and photos stay on your phone. Tillglow has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never see what you count down to.

What we collect

From your countdowns: nothing. Tillglow has no accounts, no login, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. There is no Tillglow server that receives your data.

Tillglow may offer one optional one-time upgrade ("Tillglow Pro"). In versions where that purchase is not turned on, like the first version, no purchase data exists and the purchase service is never started. When it is turned on and you buy it, the payment is processed by Google Play and the purchase is checked by RevenueCat (see Third parties). Tillglow never sees your card or payment details.

Where your data lives

Everything you create in Tillglow is stored in a database inside the app's private storage on your phone: the names, dates and times of your countdowns and count-ups, their emoji, color style, progress bar and note, your reminders, and your settings (language and reminder time).

Photos. When you add a photo to a countdown, you pick it on Android's own photo picker. Tillglow copies only that photo into its private folder, makes it smaller, and uses it on the card and the widget. Your original photo stays where it was.

Widgets. So the widgets can show the right number without opening the app, Tillglow keeps a small copy of the titles, dates and styles of your countdowns in its private storage, and the widget reads it on the phone. Widgets and reminders show your countdown titles on your phone's screen, where anyone holding your phone can see them.

Your data only leaves your phone when YOU send it:

  • Share: a countdown as text (for example "87 days until Christmas") through your phone's share menu.
  • Export CSV: a file with your countdowns that you share or save where you choose.
  • Save a backup copy: a backup file (it includes your photos) that you save wherever you choose. If it would be over 40 MB, Tillglow offers to save it without photos and says so. If you keep it in a cloud storage app, that app's own privacy terms apply; Tillglow never uploads anything itself.
  • Restore from a backup: you pick the file yourself on your phone's own screen; Tillglow can only read the file you pick.

Third parties

Your countdowns, notes and photos are never sent to anyone. Tillglow includes one third-party service, RevenueCat, used only for the optional one-time purchase. When the purchase is turned on, RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt, an anonymous app user ID and a device identifier, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. This is not linked to your name or email, is never used for tracking or advertising, and is never sold. In versions where the purchase is off, Tillglow does not start RevenueCat at all.

Tillglow includes Google's notification library but does not use push messaging; no token is created and nothing is sent. Reminders are scheduled on your phone.

After you have used Tillglow on a few different days (never on your first day), it may ask Google Play once to show its standard rating window. Any rating or review you leave goes to Google Play, not to us.

Data safety summary

Data collected: purchase history and a device or other identifier, only for the optional purchase, used only for app functionality, not shared, not used to track you, and encrypted in transit. Your countdowns, notes, photos and reminders: not collected, not shared.

How to delete your data

Your data is yours, and you can remove it at any time:

  1. On your phone: open Tillglow → Settings → Delete all my data. After two confirmations it permanently erases every countdown, reminder, photo and setting on the phone, cancels your scheduled reminders and clears the widgets' copy, so your widgets go back to "Tap to add your first countdown". The only thing it keeps is whether you bought Pro, so you don't lose your purchase. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from the phone.
  2. Files you shared or saved: backups, CSV files and texts you sent outside the app are yours; delete them where you keep them.
  3. The purchase record: if you bought Pro, email us at cmorehollman@gmail.com and we will request deletion of the anonymous purchase receipt held by RevenueCat. No name or email is attached to it. Your Google Play order history is managed in your Google account.

Children

Tillglow is made for adults. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. If a future version ever sends your countdowns, photos or any other data off your phone, we will say so here before that version is published.

Resumen en español

Tus cuentas regresivas, notas y fotos se quedan en tu teléfono. Tillglow no tiene cuentas, anuncios, analítica ni rastreo, y nosotros nunca vemos qué fechas cuentas. Todo se guarda en el almacenamiento privado de la app, incluida una copia pequeña de títulos, fechas y estilos para que los widgets muestren el número sin abrir la app. Las fotos se eligen con el selector de fotos de Android (sin permiso de galería); Tillglow copia solo la que elijas a su carpeta privada. Tus datos solo salen del teléfono cuando tú compartes una cuenta como texto, exportas el CSV o guardas una copia de seguridad (con tus fotos) donde tú elijas.

La única pieza de terceros es RevenueCat, para la compra opcional única de Pro: cuando está activa, recibe el recibo de compra, un identificador anónimo y un identificador del dispositivo, por HTTPS y sin rastreo. Si la compra no está activa, como en la primera versión, Tillglow ni siquiera inicia RevenueCat. Tillglow incluye la biblioteca de notificaciones de Google pero no usa mensajes push: no se crea ningún token y no se envía nada. Las notificaciones se piden solo cuando activas un aviso.

Para borrar todo: Tillglow → Ajustes → Borrar todos mis datos (borra cuentas, avisos, fotos y ajustes, cancela los avisos programados y deja los widgets en "Toca para agregar tu primera cuenta regresiva"; solo conserva si compraste Pro). Desinstalar la app también borra sus datos del teléfono. Si compraste Pro, escríbenos a cmorehollman@gmail.com para pedir que se borre el recibo anónimo.
