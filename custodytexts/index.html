CustodyTexts

Text messages to PDF for your lawyer or the court — from a screen recording, entirely on your phone.

Support: cmorehollman@gmail.com · TechInnova Solutions LLC

How it works

  • Record your screen while you scroll through the conversation (iMessage, SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, Android Messages).
  • CustodyTexts reads the text on your phone and places the original screen image next to every message.
  • Review each message, then export a page-numbered PDF with a cover page and an optional declaration.
  • Nothing is uploaded. One payment of US$14.99 at export, no subscription.

If something was read wrong

Open Settings → Share a diagnostic file and send it to support. It contains only the lines of text the reader saw, no images, and it lets us reproduce the exact problem.
