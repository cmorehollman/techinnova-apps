CounterStock Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026 · App: CounterStock (iPhone) · Developer: TechInnova Solutions LLC

In short: CounterStock has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your inventory and product photos are stored on your phone. We run no server and we never receive your inventory. The only outside service is RevenueCat, used to sell and verify the one-time unlock.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your inventory — shop name and currency, products (name, barcode, SKU, category, unit, quantity, cost, price, low-stock alert level), stock movements, and stock counts — is saved in a database inside the app on your phone.
  • Product photos you take or pick are reduced in size and saved in the app's own folder on your phone.
  • This data does not leave your phone unless you share a file (a CSV, a PDF or a backup file) through the share sheet, to the place you choose.
  • Like any app's data, it may be included in your own device backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), which is controlled by Apple and by you, not by us.

Camera and photo library

The camera is used to scan product barcodes and to take product photos. Barcode reading happens on the phone. You can also pick a product photo from your library; only the photo you pick is copied into the app. Camera images and photos are never sent to us.

Notifications

Low-stock alerts are local notifications created on your phone. There is no push server. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Purchases (RevenueCat)

CounterStock offers a one-time, non-consumable unlock through the App Store after a 7-day free trial. Payment is handled by Apple; we never see your card or payment details. To sell, verify and restore the unlock, the app uses RevenueCat, the only third-party service in the app. When the app opens, it checks your unlock status with RevenueCat and asks the App Store for the price. RevenueCat receives your App Store purchase and receipt information, an anonymous app user ID generated on your phone, and the technical details its software sends with each check: the app and system versions, the device model, your phone's language and store country, the connection's IP address and, on iPhone, the identifier Apple gives each app maker for your device (not the advertising identifier). This is used only to provide the purchase. It is not linked to your name or email by us, not used for advertising, not used to track you and not sold. We see purchases in RevenueCat's sales reports, without your name. RevenueCat never receives your inventory or photos. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

In a version of CounterStock that does not offer the unlock, the app makes no connection to RevenueCat or to any other service.

Internet use

The app uses the internet only for the purchase checks described above. The "Support" and "Privacy policy" links in Settings open these web pages in your browser.

App Store privacy label

  • Version with the in-app purchase: Data Not Linked to You — Purchases (your purchase history, used for app functionality and for our sales reports) and Identifiers (the anonymous user ID and the device identifier described above, used for app functionality). Not used for tracking.
  • Version without the in-app purchase: Data Not Collected.

What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in or email is required.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no sale or sharing of personal data.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings › Delete all data deletes every product, photo, movement, count and setting on the phone. Three technical records are kept, none of them inventory data: the free-trial clock (so deleting data does not restart the trial), your purchase (so you never pay twice) and a note that the app already asked for a rating once. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on the phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you saved them until you delete them. The anonymous purchase record at RevenueCat holds no inventory; to ask for its deletion, email us the order ID of the purchase (shown in your Apple purchase history) and we will ask RevenueCat to remove the matching record (the purchase can then no longer be restored on another phone).

Because we hold none of your inventory, requests to access, correct or delete personal data (for example under California, EU/EEA or UK law) are fulfilled by the steps above.

Children

CounterStock is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting something new, this page will change first and its effective date will move.
