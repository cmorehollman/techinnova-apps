What CounterStock does

  • Keeps your products, quantities, cost and price on your phone — no account, no ads, works without internet.
  • Scan barcodes to sell (−1), restock (+1), look up a product or count it. No barcode at hand? Type the code.
  • Stock counts: count everything or one category, see every difference before anything changes, then apply it.
  • Reorder list, low-stock alerts on the phone, PDF reports, CSV export and import, and backup files.

Getting started

  1. Open the app, name your shop and pick the currency.
  2. Scan your first product, add one by hand, or try the sample shop first (remove it any time in Settings; a sample product you changed stays, without the demo sales).
  3. Record sales and restocks with Scan, or from a product's page (Sell, Restock, Damaged, Adjust).

Frequently asked

How do I back up my data or move it to another phone?

Settings › Back up now creates one backup file with your products, history, counts and photos, and opens the share sheet: save it in Files, Drive or email. On the new phone, install CounterStock and, on the first screen, tap Restore from a backup file and pick that file — your shop, products, history and photos come back. On a phone already in use it is Settings › Restore from backup; restoring replaces what is on that phone with the file.

How does the price work? How do I restore my purchase?

Every feature is free for 7 days after installing. After that, adding products, recording stock changes and applying a count need a one-time purchase — no subscription. Viewing your stock, editing product details, CSV and PDF exports, and backup and restore stay free. If you reinstall the app or use a new iPhone with the same Apple Account, open Settings › Your plan › Restore purchase; you are not charged again.

My CSV from Excel shows strange codes like 7.50123E+12.

A spreadsheet can rewrite long barcodes in scientific notation and lose their digits. The import lists those rows as problems and does not change your products. In the spreadsheet, format the barcode column as Text, fix the codes and import again. If accents look wrong, save the file as "CSV UTF-8".

The scanner does not open the camera.

Allow the camera in iPhone Settings › CounterStock › Camera. You can always type a code in the scanner instead.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Delete all data. See the Privacy Policy for what is kept (the trial clock and your purchase).
