Your way to the Oath. Study the U.S. civics test calmly.

Practice the civics questions of the 2025 test (128) or the 2008 test (100), hear them read aloud by your phone's voice, take mock tests with the rules USCIS describes, and keep the answers that change (senators, governor, President) up to date for your state, with the date we checked them.

Oathway is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to USCIS or any government agency. Questions and answers come from USCIS at uscis.gov/citizenship. Always check uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates for answers that change.

How to use Oathway

  1. Pick your test and your state.
  2. Study the questions and listen to them.
  3. Take mock tests until you feel calm.

Questions people ask

Is Oathway from the government?

No. Oathway is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with USCIS. The questions come from uscis.gov.

Which test will I take?

If you filed your N-400 before October 20, 2025, you take the 2008 test. If you filed on or after that date, you take the 2025 test. Use the "Received Date" on your I-797C receipt notice. You can change the test anytime in Settings.

Which name do I say for senators, governor or President?

The person in office on the day of your interview. Oathway shows the date we checked each name, and you can edit it in My answers.

Does Oathway sync or need an account?

No, on purpose. Everything is saved on your phone. Use Settings → Save a backup to move your progress to a new phone.

Does it work without internet?

Yes for studying. Voices marked "needs internet" won't play offline.

The voice sounds wrong or there is no voice

Open Settings → Voice. Try another voice, or install voice data in your phone settings (Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech).

How do I restore my purchase?

Oathway 1.0.0 has no purchases: everything is included. If an optional Pro purchase is offered in a later version: Settings → Your plan → Restore purchase, with the same Google account you used to buy.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete all my data. See the Privacy Policy for all the steps.
