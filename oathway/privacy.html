Oathway — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026 · App version 1.0.0

The short version

Your progress, mock tests, saved questions, edited answers and settings stay on your phone. Oathway has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We (TechInnova Solutions LLC) never receive your study data.

What we collect

Your study data: nothing. Oathway has no sign-up and no login, and it does not send your progress or answers to us or to anyone else.

Purchases: Oathway may later offer one optional one-time purchase ("Pro"). In version 1.0.0 purchases are turned off, the purchase service is not started, and no purchase data exists. If purchases are turned on in a later version, the payment is handled by Google Play and the purchase is checked by RevenueCat (see Third parties). We will update this page before that version is published.

Where your data lives

Everything you do in Oathway is saved in a database inside the app's private storage on your phone:

  • the test you study (2025 or 2008), 65/20 mode, and the N-400 filing date if you enter it;
  • the questions you practiced, your right and missed answers, saved questions and mock test results;
  • your state, the answers you edit and the representative you type;
  • your interview date if you add one, and your reading and writing practice;
  • your settings (language, voice, speed).

Android's automatic app backup is turned off for Oathway, so this data is not copied to your Google account. It leaves your phone only if you save a backup file (Oathway-backup.json) or export your progress (Oathway-Progress-YYYY-MM.csv) and share it yourself with the Android share menu. When you restore a backup, Oathway reads the file you pick on your phone and does not upload it.

Speech (voices)

Oathway reads questions aloud with your phone's own text-to-speech. Only question and answer text from the app is spoken. If you choose a voice marked "needs internet", your phone's speech service may send that text to its provider to create the audio. Oathway never sends your progress or your answers.

Links

Links to uscis.gov, senate.gov, house.gov and usa.gov open in your browser. The contact link opens your email app. Those sites and apps have their own privacy policies.

Third parties

  • Google Play distributes the app. If purchases are turned on in a later version, Google Play handles the payment.
  • RevenueCat (the purchase service) is included in the app but is not started in version 1.0.0. If purchases are turned on, it receives the purchase receipt, an anonymous app user ID and a device identifier, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, only to confirm the purchase. It is not used for advertising or tracking, and we never sell it.

There are no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services in Oathway.

Data safety summary

Declared in Google Play: purchase history (optional, only if you buy Pro) and device or other IDs (the anonymous purchase ID), both collected only for app functionality, not shared, and encrypted in transit. We declare them from version 1.0.0 because the purchase service is inside the app, even though it is off. Nothing else is collected or shared.

How to delete your data

  1. On your phone: open Oathway → Settings → Delete all my data. This erases your progress, mock tests, edited answers and settings on the phone, removes the export files Oathway left in its storage, and starts setup again. Uninstalling the app also removes its data. Backup or CSV files you shared yourself stay wherever you put them.
  2. A purchase record (only if you bought Pro in a later version): email cmorehollman@gmail.com and we will ask RevenueCat to delete the anonymous purchase record. Google Play keeps its own order history under Google's policy.

Not a government app

Oathway is an independent study app made by TechInnova Solutions LLC. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to USCIS or any government agency. The civics questions and answers come from USCIS at uscis.gov/citizenship. The Spanish translations are unofficial and made by Oathway.

Children

Oathway is made for adults getting ready for the naturalization interview. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If what Oathway does with data changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before the new version is published.
