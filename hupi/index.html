Need help? Write to cmorehollman@gmail.com. Tell us your device (for example "iPhone 13, iOS 18") and what happened. We never ask for your children's names, photos or your grown-up PIN.

Leaving Kid Mode

Tap “Grown-up” at the top left. With a grown-up PIN, type it. Without a PIN, Hupi asks a multiplication written in words (for example “What is fourteen times seven?”): type the answer in digits and tap “Check”. With a PIN set, if Hupi is closed while it is in Kid Mode, it opens in Kid Mode again.

I forgot my PIN

On the PIN screen, press and hold “Forgot PIN?” for 5 seconds, then answer the multiplication written in words. Hupi then asks you to choose a new PIN.

Moving to a new phone or tablet

In Hupi, open Settings and tap “Save a backup file”, then keep the file (email it to yourself, or save it to Files or Drive). On the new device, install Hupi and choose “Restore from a backup file” (in Settings, or on the first screen). The file includes the photos. Restoring replaces the children, routines and history on that device. If the file has a grown-up PIN, it replaces the device's PIN; if it has none, the device keeps its PIN.

A Done tapped by mistake

In Kid Mode, “↶ Undo” at the top right takes back the last Done, so History shows only what really happened.

What is free

History, printing picture charts, the CSV export, backups and restoring are always free. If your version of Hupi offers a one-time unlock, the app shows its terms and price before the free trial starts (onboarding and Settings › Your plan); “Restore purchase” in Settings brings back a purchase you already made.

Not a medical tool

Hupi is an organizing tool for daily routines. It is not a medical device and gives no medical advice.
