Hupi — Privacy Policy

Hupi: Kids Visual Schedule · TechInnova Solutions LLC · Effective September 29, 2026

In short: Hupi has no account, no ads, no usage analytics and no tracking. The children, routines, photos and history you create stay on your device. We do not receive them. The only outside service Hupi may talk to is RevenueCat, and only where Hupi offers an in-app purchase: it checks the purchase with the App Store or Google Play using an anonymous ID. A version without purchases talks to no outside service.

What Hupi stores, and where

  • Children: a first name (or nickname), an emoji or photo, and a color.
  • Routines and steps: titles, labels, emoji or photos, days of the week, usual times and timers.
  • History: which routine was started, when each step was marked done, when it finished, and the device's time zone at that moment (so the times stay right after a trip).
  • Settings: your grown-up PIN (if you set one), sound and screen-on preferences.
  • Small technical notes Hupi needs to work: which child was selected last, whether setup is finished, free-trial timing and unlock status, wrong PIN tries, and when Hupi last asked for a rating.

All of this is saved only in Hupi's private storage on your device. Photos you add are resized and copied into Hupi; they are not saved to your photo library and are not uploaded anywhere by us.

On iPhone and iPad, the device's own iCloud or computer backup can include Hupi's data, like any app's data, so a restored device brings your routines back. On Android, Hupi's data is excluded from the system backup; use Hupi's own backup file instead.

Hupi does not ask for, and has no field for, any health information, diagnosis or medication.

Camera and photo library

Hupi uses the camera only when you tap "Take photo" for a step or a child's picture, and opens your photo library only when you tap "Choose photo". Hupi never records audio and does not use the microphone.

Files you choose to share

When you print a picture chart (PDF), export the history (CSV) or save a backup file, Hupi creates the file on your device and opens your device's share sheet. Where the file goes next (email, Files, a printer, a cloud drive) is your choice and is governed by that service. A backup file contains your children's names, routines, photos, history and your grown-up PIN, so keep it somewhere private.

Purchases (RevenueCat)

If Hupi offers an in-app purchase on your device, it uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) to check the purchase with the App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID it creates, the device's purchase receipt and purchase history, and basic device information needed to validate the purchase (such as an identifier for the app vendor). No name, email, photo, routine or history is sent. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

Hupi is set up and managed by a parent or other grown-up. Hupi does not collect personal information from children, has no chat, no sharing between users and no ads, and keeps settings and purchases behind the grown-up PIN when one is set.

Deleting your data

Settings ▸ "Delete all my data" deletes every child, routine, photo and history day from the device, together with the files Hupi created in its cache. Deleting the app also deletes its data. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

App Store privacy label

A version of Hupi without in-app purchases: Data Not Collected. A version with the one-time unlock: Data Not Linked to You — Purchases (purchase history) and Identifiers (the anonymous RevenueCat user ID and a device identifier), used to make the purchase work and to count sales in RevenueCat's dashboard. No data is used to track you, and none of it is your children's names, photos, routines or history.
