CustodyBook — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

The short version: your custody record stays on your phone unless you share it. Some phones can also copy app data on their own, as explained below. If you buy CustodyBook, the purchase leaves records with the store or with our payment services, also explained below.

What we collect

Your record: nothing. CustodyBook has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking of our own. If you buy the one-time unlock, the purchase itself is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) and validated by RevenueCat (see below). If you buy CustodyBook on our website instead, that payment is processed by Stripe through RevenueCat, and the app unlocks when you open the redemption link from your purchase email. For a website purchase we keep your email, what you bought, the price and the date, and RevenueCat can send Meta the purchase with your email in hashed form so we can measure our ads. Our website privacy policy explains this and how to opt out.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter is stored locally on your device. It stays on your phone unless you export it (CSV, PDF or backup file) and share it yourself. The other parent never sees it unless you share it. Separately, some phones can copy app data on their own, in a phone backup or when you move to a new phone. That depends on the phone, its settings and the version of CustodyBook. Any such copy is kept under the rules of the service you use for it, not by us.

Third parties

The app does not send your entries to us or to anyone else unless you share them.

RevenueCat is used only for the one-time purchase: to show the price, validate it and check whether it is unlocked. The app can contact it even if you haven't bought. It receives the purchase receipt (or, for a website purchase, the one-time redemption code in your link), an app user ID that RevenueCat creates (random, not your name or email) and technical details about the app and device, which can include a device identifier. The app does not give RevenueCat your name, your email or your entries. We do not sell this data. A website purchase is tied to the email you used at checkout, as explained above. If redeeming a link fails, the app may check whether RevenueCat's server is reachable; that check includes nothing you entered.

Google ML Kit (Android only) reads screen recordings for Messages, on the phone. Google says ML Kit can collect device and app information, a per-installation identifier, performance measures and usage details, for diagnostics and usage analytics. Its list does not include the images or text it reads.

Like any developer, we get the stores' sales reports, install counts, usage and crash statistics, and any review you choose to post. A sales report can include an order number, the date, the price, the device model and the buyer's city, state, postal code and country, depending on the store. It does not include anything you enter in CustodyBook.

Store privacy summaries

The App Store and Google Play each show their own privacy summary for CustodyBook. This policy gives the details.

Your rights

"Delete ALL my data" in Settings erases what the app keeps on this device: entries, photos, saved messages, child info, and its copies of your backups and reports. It keeps your purchase status and a few app notes. Deleting the app removes its data from the phone. Files you sent or saved somewhere else, and any copy your phone made on its own, are not touched. To ask about what we hold for a website purchase, write to us. Export your data any time from Settings.
