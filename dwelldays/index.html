Common questions

How does a day count?

A day counts for every place you slept in or were also in, once per date. Any part of a day counts as a day. Your state's rules may differ.

What is the 183 line?

A reference mark for each state you watch. It is a mark, never a conclusion. The tax authority decides what it accepts.

How do I move my log to a new phone?

Open Menu → Copies → Make a copy and send the file to yourself (for example by email or Drive). On the new phone, open Menu → Copies → Bring a copy back. If you bought the app, also use Restore purchase.

How do I restore my purchase?

Install the app with the same Google (or Apple) account, open Menu → Buy the app, and tap Restore purchase.
