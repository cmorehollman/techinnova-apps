Dwelldays — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026 · TechInnova Solutions

The short version: there is nothing to sign up for, the app never asks for your location or your photos, and it has no analytics. Your log stays on your phone unless you share a file.

What the app stores

The days you log (where you slept and other places you were that day), your notes, your home state and the states you watch, the date each day was logged and its edits, whether you bought the app, and your settings. All of it is stored in the app on your device. We have no server for your log and we never receive it.

When data leaves your phone

  • Only when you share a file. A year PDF, a CSV or a copy (backup) leaves your phone only when you choose to share it through your phone's share sheet (for example to Drive, Gmail or Files). Where it goes after that is up to you and that service.
  • The one-time purchase. Buying or restoring Dwelldays is handled by Google Play (on Android) or Apple (on iPhone). The app never receives your card or payment details. Like any developer, we may see the store's sales reports, which do not include your card. The store's own privacy policy covers that purchase.
  • Your phone's backup. If you have Android's backup to your Google account turned on, Android may include the app's data in your own backup. Your phone settings and Google control that, not us.
  • Ratings. The app may show the store's own rating window. If you rate the app, the store handles it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your location, photos, contacts or microphone. Logging is manual.
  • It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not sell or share data.
  • It does not use push messages from a server.
  • It only reads a file when you pick one yourself (Compare a CSV, Bring a copy back), and that file stays on your phone.
  • On Android, when you share a file, a temporary copy stays in the app's private storage for at least a day so the receiving app can read it; the app deletes it the next time it starts or exports after that.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules the reminder on your phone. Nothing about it is sent to us. You can turn it off in the app or in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

Use Menu → Copies → Erase everything (it asks you to confirm twice), or uninstall the app: your log is deleted from the phone. Files you already shared, and your own phone backup, stay where they are.

Children

Dwelldays is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
