Envelo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

The short version: your budget stays on your phone unless you export it and share it. Some phones can also copy app data on their own, as explained below. If you buy Envelo, the purchase leaves a receipt and a store sales report, also explained below.

What we collect

Your budget: nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking of any kind. The stores give us their own reports, explained under Third parties. If you buy the one-time unlock, the purchase itself is processed by Apple or Google and validated by RevenueCat (see below).

Where your data lives

Every envelope, transaction and setting is stored locally on your device, and so are the automatic backups Envelo makes. It stays on your phone unless you export it (CSV or backup file) and share it yourself. Separately, some phones can copy app data on their own, in a phone backup or when you move to a new phone. That depends on the phone, its settings and the version of Envelo. Any such copy is kept under the rules of the service you use for it, not by us.

Third parties

The app does not send your entries to us or to anyone else unless you share them. The app includes one third-party SDK, RevenueCat, used only for the one-time purchase: to show the price, validate it and check whether it is unlocked. The app can contact it even if you haven't bought. It receives the purchase receipt, an anonymous user ID and technical details about the app and device, which can include a device identifier. The app does not give RevenueCat your name, your email or your entries. We do not sell this data.

Like any developer, we get the store's sales reports, install counts, usage and crash statistics, and any review you choose to post. A sales report can include an order number, the date, the price, the device model and the buyer's city, state, postal code and country, depending on the store. It does not include anything you enter in Envelo.

App Store privacy label

"Data Not Linked to You": purchase history and identifiers (anonymous), used only for app functionality. Everything else: not collected.

Your rights

Your data is yours. "Delete ALL my data" in Settings erases your envelopes, movements and debts from this phone. Depending on the version of Envelo, it also erases the automatic backups on the phone, or they stay until you delete the app. It keeps a few app notes, such as your purchase status and trial dates. Deleting the app removes its data from the phone. Copies you saved or shared, and any copy your phone made on its own, are not touched. Export your data any time from Settings.
