HourTab

A weekly time card for people paid by the hour

No accountNo adsYour hours stay on your phoneEnglish · Español

HourTab adds up your week the way your paycheck should: In, Out and unpaid break for each day, a total in hours and minutes (8:30) and in decimal hours (8.50), regular hours, overtime and your estimated gross pay. It is your own record to check your pay — not your employer's time clock.

How to use HourTab

  1. Type your In and Out times in the week card — 830 means 8:30 — and tap AM or PM under the day. Every change saves as you type.
  2. Or tap Clock in when you start and Clock out when you finish. Breaks you start with Start break are not counted as work.
  3. Your total, regular hours, overtime and estimated gross pay add up at the bottom of the week.
  4. Every week stays in History. Tap a week to open it and fix it.
  5. Export any week as a PDF or CSV timesheet, and keep a backup copy from Settings → Backups and restore.

FAQ

Does it sync or back up to the cloud?

No, on purpose: there is no account and your hours stay on your phone. To keep a copy off the phone, pick a backup folder or save a backup file in Settings → Backups and restore.

How is overtime counted?

Weekly overtime after 40 hours by default. Daily overtime and double time can be turned on in your job settings, where you also set the limits and the multipliers. Hours already counted as daily overtime don't count again toward the weekly 40.

My shift goes past midnight.

Just type the times. HourTab shows "+1" and counts the whole shift on the day it started.

AM or PM came out wrong.

Tap AM or PM under the day you are typing in. HourTab learns your usual In and Out times for each job, so next time it guesses right. 12-hour shifts like 7a–7:30p are understood.

Can I save my usual shift?

Yes, automatically: after you work the same shift twice, it shows up as a "Usual shifts" chip on an empty day. One tap fills it.

I got a raise.

Change the hourly pay in your job settings and pick the week it starts. Earlier weeks keep the old pay.

I changed jobs.

Job settings → I changed jobs. Your old job is archived and its hours stay in History and exports.

Can my employer see my hours?

No. Nobody sees your hours unless you share a PDF or CSV yourself.

How do I move to a new phone?

Settings → Backups and restore → Save a backup copy. On the new phone, install HourTab and use Restore from a file.

How do I restore a purchase?

HourTab is free to use. If you ever buy the optional Pro upgrade, you can restore it from Settings → Your plan → Restore purchase, using the same Google account.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete all my data, or email us. Full steps are in the privacy policy.

Is this payroll, tax or legal advice?

No. HourTab gives estimates to help you check your pay. Overtime rules vary by state and employer.
