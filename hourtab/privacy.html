HourTab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

The short version

Your work hours stay on your phone. HourTab has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never see your hours, your pay or your notes.

What we collect

Your hours, jobs and pay: nothing. HourTab has no accounts, no login, no analytics, no ads and no tracking of any kind. There is no HourTab server that receives your data.

HourTab may offer one optional one-time purchase ("Pro"). In the current version that purchase is not turned on, so no purchase data exists. When it is turned on, the payment is processed by Google Play and the purchase is checked by RevenueCat (see Third parties).

Where your data lives

Everything you enter — shifts, breaks, jobs, hourly pay and pay history, overtime and rounding settings, day notes, your name if you add it, and the employer details you type for a timesheet (company, supervisor, employee ID) — is stored in a database inside the app's private storage on your phone. The automatic copies HourTab makes (the last 7) are kept there too.

Your data only leaves your phone when YOU do it:

  • Export: a PDF or CSV timesheet you share through your phone's share menu (email, Drive, a message, a printer…).
  • Save a backup copy: a backup file you send wherever you choose.
  • Backup folder (optional): if you pick a folder, HourTab writes its backup copies there. If that folder is synced by a cloud app, that app's own privacy terms apply; HourTab never uploads anything itself.

HourTab is your personal record, not your employer's time clock. Your employer does not see your hours unless you share a file with them.

Third parties

Your hours are never sent to anyone. The app includes one third-party SDK, RevenueCat, used only for the optional one-time purchase. When the purchase is turned on, RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt, an anonymous app user ID and a device identifier, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. This is not linked to your identity, is never used for tracking or advertising, and is never sold. In the current version the purchase is off and RevenueCat is not started.

Once, after you have used HourTab for a while (never on your first day), it may ask Google Play to show its standard rating window. Any rating or review you leave goes to Google Play, not to us.

Permissions

HourTab uses internet access (only for the optional purchase), Google Play billing (for that purchase) and vibration (a short tap on a few actions, like clocking in or out). It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, calendar or notifications. To restore a backup or choose a backup folder, you pick the file or folder yourself on your phone's own screen; HourTab can only reach what you pick.

The app package also lists two entries that come with standard libraries: network state (lets those libraries check whether the phone is online) and the Google Play install referrer service. HourTab never reads the install referrer and does not measure where your download came from.

Data safety summary

Data collected: purchase history and a device or other identifier, only for the optional purchase, used only for app functionality, not shared, not used to track you, and encrypted in transit. Your hours and everything else: not collected, not shared.

How to delete your data

Your data is yours, and you can remove it at any time:

  1. On your phone: open HourTab → Settings → Delete all my data. After two confirmations it permanently erases every shift, job, note, setting and automatic copy on the phone. If you use a backup folder, the second step has a switch (on by default) to also delete the HourTab backup copies in that folder; HourTab only deletes its own HourTab-backup-…json files there, never other files. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from the phone.
  2. Files you exported or saved: PDFs, CSVs and backup files you shared or saved outside the app are yours; delete them where you keep them.
  3. The purchase record: if you bought Pro, email us at cmorehollman@gmail.com and we will request deletion of the anonymous purchase receipt held by RevenueCat. No personal identity is attached to it. Your Google Play order history is managed in your Google account.

Estimates, not advice

HourTab gives estimates to help you check your pay. It isn't payroll, tax or legal advice. Overtime rules vary by state and employer.

Children

HourTab is made for adults who work for pay. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. If a future version ever sends your hours or any other data off your phone, we will say so here before that version is published.

Resumen en español

Tus horas de trabajo se quedan en tu teléfono. HourTab no tiene cuentas, anuncios, analítica ni rastreo, y nosotros nunca vemos tus horas, tu pago ni tus notas. Tus turnos, trabajos, pagos, notas, tu nombre (si lo pones) y los datos del empleador que escribas para una hoja de horas se guardan solo en tu teléfono, junto con las 7 copias automáticas. Solo salen de tu teléfono si tú los exportas (PDF o CSV), guardas una copia o eliges una carpeta de copias; si esa carpeta la sincroniza una app de nube, se aplican las condiciones de esa app. La única pieza de terceros es RevenueCat, para la compra opcional de Pro (apagada en esta versión): cuando se active, recibe el recibo de compra, un identificador anónimo y un identificador del dispositivo, por HTTPS y sin rastreo.

Para borrar todo: HourTab → Ajustes → Borrar todos mis datos (borra turnos, trabajos, notas, ajustes y copias automáticas; un interruptor, encendido por defecto, borra también las copias de HourTab de tu carpeta de copias). Desinstalar la app también borra sus datos del teléfono. Si compraste Pro, escríbenos a cmorehollman@gmail.com para pedir que se borre el recibo anónimo.
