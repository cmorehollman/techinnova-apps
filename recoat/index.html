Take or choose a photo, tap a wall and try colors on it. Add trim, door and shutters, mark what should stay as it is (windows, brick, the roof), and save the picture with a color card to take to the paint store. No photo yet? Start with the sample house or the sample room in the app.

How to use Recoat

  1. Choose a photo — your house from the street, or a room. The whole wall in the picture works well.
  2. Tap the wall. Smart tap paints the wall you touch and stops at its edges.
  3. Fix the edges. Move Edge sensitivity, or use lasso, polygon, brush and eraser. Pinch to zoom in.
  4. Add trim and door. Tap "+ Surface" for trim, door or shutters. Add Keep as is over windows, brick or the roof so they never get painted.
  5. Save and take it to the store. Save the image with its color card. The hex code and LRV help you compare real paint samples.

Tips for better photos

  • Even daylight works well. Cloudy days are great.
  • Avoid hard shadows across the wall.
  • Stand in front of the wall, not at an angle, and get the whole wall in the photo.
  • In rooms, turn on all the lights.
  • On brick, busy textures or white trim on a white wall, outline the wall with the polygon tool.

Questions people ask

Does it sync?

No, on purpose. Your photos and projects stay on this phone. Use Settings → Back up everything to move them to a new phone, then Settings → Restore from backup there.

How do I stop it from painting my windows or door?

Add Keep as is and tap or draw over them. Smart tap never paints there. It's free.

It painted too much (or too little). What do I do?

Tap Undo, lower (or raise) Edge sensitivity and tap again. For the last details use lasso, polygon, brush or eraser. If a spot already belongs to another surface, Recoat tells you and offers to paint it as the surface you're working on.

Are these real paint brand colors?

No. Recoat shows any color you pick by hex code. It is not matched to any paint brand. Always test a real sample. In My colors you can write the name or code from your paint sample.

Why does my wall look different from the sample?

Screens, cameras and light change colors. Use the photo only to compare ideas.

What is LRV?

Light reflectance value: how much light a color reflects, from 0 (black) to 100 (white). Recoat estimates it from the screen color; paint makers measure it on real paint.

Where are my saved images?

In the folder you picked the first time you tapped Save to phone. If you picked Pictures, they also show up in your gallery. You can change the folder on the Save & share screen.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete all my data. It removes every project, photo and saved color from this phone. See the Privacy Policy for full steps.
