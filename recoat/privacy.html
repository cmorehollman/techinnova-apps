Recoat — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

The short version: your photos never leave your phone unless you share them. Recoat has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never see your photos, the colors you try or the notes you write.

What we collect

From your photos, projects and colors: nothing. Recoat has no accounts, no login, no analytics, no ads and no tracking of any kind. There is no Recoat server that receives your photos or your data. Recoat changes the colors of your photo on your phone; the photo is never uploaded anywhere to be processed.

Recoat may offer one optional one-time purchase ("Pro"). In the current version that purchase is not turned on, so no purchase data exists. When it is turned on, the payment is processed by Google Play and the purchase is checked by RevenueCat (see Third parties).

Where your data lives

Everything you make in the app is stored in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • a working copy of each photo you pick or take (reduced to 1,600 pixels on the long side) and a small thumbnail;
  • the painted areas, surfaces, color schemes and the colors you choose;
  • My colors, with the names and notes you type (for example, the code of a paint sample);
  • your settings (language, editor options).

While a photo is imported or a backup is restored, Android keeps temporary copies in the app's cache. Recoat removes them when they are no longer needed, and Delete all my data removes any that remain.

Your data only goes somewhere else when YOU do it:

  • Save to phone: the image is written to a folder you pick (Pictures is suggested, so it shows up in your gallery). Other apps on your phone that can see that folder, like a gallery or a photo backup app, can see it too; their own privacy terms apply.
  • Share image: the image goes where you send it with your phone's share menu (a message, email, Drive…).
  • Export color list (CSV): a file with your colors, their hex codes and your color names and notes, shared the same way.
  • Back up everything: a file with all your projects, including your photos, painted areas and colors. Keep it somewhere private.

The images Recoat saves or shares are newly drawn by the app: they do not include your original photo's location or camera details.

Camera and photos

Camera (optional): used only when you tap "Take a photo", to take the picture you want to paint. Recoat explains this before Android asks for permission. You can say no and still use Recoat with a photo you choose or with the sample house and sample room.

Your photos: Recoat uses your phone's own photo picker. It only receives the one photo you choose. It never has access to your whole gallery and does not ask for storage or media permissions.

Third parties

Your photos and projects are never sent to anyone. The app includes one third-party SDK, RevenueCat, used only for the optional one-time purchase. When the purchase is turned on, RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt, an anonymous app user ID and a device identifier, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. This is not linked to your identity, is never used for tracking or advertising, and is never sold. In the current version the purchase is off and RevenueCat is not started.

Once, after you have used Recoat on at least two different days (never on your first day), it may ask Google Play to show its standard rating window. Any rating or review you leave goes to Google Play, not to us.

Permissions

Recoat uses the camera (optional, see above; the app also installs on devices without a camera), internet access (only for the optional purchase), Google Play billing (for that purchase) and vibration (a short tap when a wall is painted or a color is set). It does not use your location, contacts, microphone, notifications or your photo gallery. To save an image, restore a backup or pick a folder, you choose the file or folder yourself on your phone's own screen; Recoat can only reach what you pick.

The app package also lists two entries that come with standard libraries: network state (lets those libraries check whether the phone is online) and the Google Play install referrer service. Recoat never reads the install referrer and does not measure where your download came from.

Data safety summary

Data collected: purchase history and a device or other identifier, only for the optional purchase, used only for app functionality, not shared, not used to track you, and encrypted in transit. Your photos, projects, colors and everything else: not collected, not shared.

How to delete your data

Your data is yours, and you can remove it at any time:

  1. On your phone: open Recoat → Settings → Delete all my data. After you confirm, it permanently erases every project, photo, painted area, color scheme, saved color and setting in the app, plus the temporary copies in its cache, and the app starts over. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from the phone.
  2. Files you saved or shared: images in your Pictures folder (or the folder you picked), backup files and CSV files are yours; delete them where you keep them. If you picked a folder for "Save to phone", Android may keep Recoat's access to that folder until you uninstall the app; Recoat forgets which folder it was when you delete your data.
  3. The purchase record: if you bought Pro, email us at cmorehollman@gmail.com and we will request deletion of the anonymous purchase receipt held by RevenueCat. No personal identity is attached to it. Your Google Play order history is managed in your Google account.

Colors are a preview

Recoat shows colors on your photo so you can compare ideas. Screens, cameras and light change how colors look, and the colors are not matched to any paint brand. Always check a real paint sample.

Children

Recoat is made for adults planning a paint project. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. If a future version ever sends your photos or any other data off your phone, we will say so here before that version is published.

Resumen en español

Tus fotos nunca salen de tu teléfono salvo que tú las compartas. Recoat no tiene cuentas, anuncios, analítica ni rastreo, y nosotros nunca vemos tus fotos, los colores que pruebas ni tus notas. La copia de trabajo de cada foto, las zonas pintadas, las combinaciones, Mis colores (con sus nombres y notas) y los ajustes se guardan solo en tu teléfono. La cámara se usa únicamente para tomar la foto que quieres pintar (puedes decir que no), y las fotos se eligen con el selector del sistema: Recoat recibe solo la foto que eliges, nunca toda tu galería. Tus datos solo salen cuando tú guardas una imagen en una carpeta, la compartes, exportas el CSV o haces un respaldo (el respaldo lleva tus fotos: guárdalo en un lugar privado). Las imágenes que guarda o comparte Recoat no llevan la ubicación ni los datos de la cámara de tu foto original. La única pieza de terceros es RevenueCat, para la compra opcional de Pro (apagada en esta versión): cuando se active, recibe el recibo de compra, un identificador anónimo y un identificador del dispositivo, por HTTPS y sin rastreo.

Para borrar todo: Recoat → Ajustes → Borrar todos mis datos (borra proyectos, fotos, zonas pintadas, combinaciones, colores guardados, ajustes y las copias temporales). Desinstalar la app también borra sus datos del teléfono. Las imágenes, respaldos y CSV que guardaste fuera de la app son tuyos: bórralos donde los tengas. Si compraste Pro, escríbenos a cmorehollman@gmail.com para pedir que se borre el recibo anónimo.
