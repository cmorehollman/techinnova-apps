Quick help

  • Planting calendar: choose your frost zone or type your last-frost date (MM-DD) in Settings. Dates are estimates; check your local conditions.
  • Beds and plantings: create a bed, add a planting from a seed packet, then log sowed, germinated, transplanted and harvested.
  • Season summary: see every bed and planting, and export it as PDF or CSV. Export is always free.
  • Backup: Settings → save a backup file; restore it on a new phone from the same screen.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete all my data.
